Two pillars. One platform.

Friction-anchor interaction

The robot presses against a surface and uses friction to support part of its load. We develop its flight and contact control together.

Soft-optical tactile sensing

A camera inside a soft sensor observes its deformation. A learned model estimates contact force and surface orientation from those images.

What the evidence supports

The two pillars have been combined in laboratory contact-flight experiments. Performance on field surfaces and in operational conditions remains to be established.
