SOAR-NDT

Developing a tactile aerial tool for non-destructive steel thickness inspection.

In development

Contact is
the starting point.

The project explores combining Volar's two pillars with a pulsed eddy current probe. The aim is to position a thickness measurement tool against hard-to-reach steel surfaces.

What still needs validation?

The integrated tactile and inspection tool, measurement quality, and repeatability need validation. Laboratory contact flight does not establish an operational inspection capability.

About the program

SOAR-NDT was selected in AUTOASSESS Open Call #2. The program's public description sets out the intended inspection application.
